# Roadmap

## Milestone 0 — Vertical Slice (current)

One Island X sector map, casual 1v1 and 3v3, one weapon per slot plus knife, movement, gunplay, health/shields, full round loop with overtime, and the three class abilities. See the Vertical Slice Amendment in MASTERPLAN.md.

## Milestone 1 — Combat Depth

Remaining weapon archetypes, special/heavy ammo economy on the map, grenades, 5v5.

## Milestone 2 — Public Beta Maps

Cryo Foundry, Neon Divide, Overflow; map rotation and Island X map selection.

## Milestone 3 — Ranked

Queues, ELO, persistence, abandonment handling.

## Milestone 4 — Cosmetics and Store

Inventory, locker, store, secure purchases, soft currency, emotes and communication.

## Milestone 5 — Seasons

Seasonal progression per the Seasonal Progression Amendment.

## Far Future (explicitly not planned)

Battle royale on the full Island X.
